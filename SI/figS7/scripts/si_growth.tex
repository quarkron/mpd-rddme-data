isometric projection, drawn back to front so that nearer strands overlay farther ones. The leftmost
is the seed, a three-cycle, which is planar and therefore unknotted; each later panel is the
previous one plus insertions. Closure, self-avoidance, the parity class and the knot type hold at
every intermediate length, so no panel needs checking and none was checked. The compaction visible
by $N=60$ is why the procedure interleaves relaxation with growth rather than leaving it to the
end.}
